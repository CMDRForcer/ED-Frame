# ED-Frame 1.0.13

Clear material swaps for your entire build, faster background uploads and station-market refresh through Frontier CAPI.

## What's new

- **Full-build material trades:** Operations once again shows the complete planned trade list beside the trader route. All material categories appear by default. Each row tells you exactly what to **give** and **receive**, including quantities, trader location and unavoidable surplus. Long names wrap, and materials reserved by the build remain protected. Next Best Action can focus on one module without hiding the rest of the build's trades.
- **Docked station markets through Frontier:** refresh your profile, shipyard and station market together. Validated public market observations can enter the existing EDDN queue when Frontier consent and EDDN uploads are enabled. Connections shows offer counts, observation time and delivery state. Missing or incomplete responses cannot erase an inventory; newer Journal observations remain authoritative. No extra polling timer is added.
- **An independent upload lane:** other searches and catalog imports no longer occupy EDDN's single upload worker. Station inventories take priority over signal backlogs, preserving order within each group. Existing send intervals, Commander checks, consent and server cooldowns still apply.
- **Durable receipts and quieter updates:** acknowledgements and retries are recorded before the UI callback, accepted messages are reconciled on restart, and UI/JSON updates are batched. Bulk archival runs in the background. Connections → EDDN → Show technical details now includes local sender timings. These changes remove identified upload bottlenecks; they do not promise zero UI pauses or a fixed live upload speed.
- **Server maintenance sources:** the repository includes the tested capacity-aware online index-maintenance worker used after the October outage. It compacts selected secondary indexes with backup and disk-space checks, retaining catalog facts and backups. Catalog API **0.9.5** remains compatible; this app update needs no further server deployment.

An EDDN acceptance confirms gateway delivery. Inara and community bots ingest data independently; their update time is not guaranteed by that receipt.

## Install

From **1.0.11 or later**, use **Settings → About**, or extract the complete **ED-Frame-1.0.13-Windows.zip** into a new writable folder. Keep `_internal` next to `ED-Frame.exe`. Fully exit the previous app, including its tray process, before starting the new version. Older versions require manual installation. Existing AppData profiles, builds and credentials remain in place; keep your usual backups.

The release workflow runs the complete application suite, repository privacy checks, source and packaged QML smoke tests, update/rollback verification, manifest checks and archive checksums before publishing downloads. The private administrator tool and personal profiles are excluded.

## Deutsch

- **Materialtausch für den gesamten Build:** alle Kategorien sowie klare Zeilen **Abgeben → Erhalten** mit Material, Menge, Händler und Überschuss. Der Layoutfehler der unsichtbaren Tauschkarte ist behoben; für den Build reservierte Bestände bleiben geschützt.

- **Schnellere Warteschlange:** eigener serieller Upload-Worker, Stationsangebote vor Signal-Rückstau, dauerhaft gespeicherte Quittungen und gebündelte UI-Aktualisierungen. Sendelimits und Einwilligungen bleiben wirksam.

Ab **1.0.11** über **Settings → About** aktualisieren. Alternativ das vollständige Windows-Paket neu entpacken und die alte App einschließlich Tray beenden. Der vorhandene Profilordner wird weiterverwendet; kein zusätzlicher Serverumbau ist nötig.
