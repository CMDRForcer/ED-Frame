# ED-Frame 1.0.8 — more Mining results and bounded background work

Mining searches now supplement regional price top lists with grouped exact
system/commodity requests. The matching catalog server filters Powerplay pages
by your chosen Power and goal before pagination, so unrelated observations do
not crowd suitable systems out of a wide search. Existing servers retain the
previous bounded exact-query fallback.
The grouped response also recovers exact rings omitted by regional read limits.

Powerplay observations up to 48 hours old are treated as current. Eligible
observations up to 14 days old remain useful as explicitly dated **last known /
check in game** proposals. They never count as verified merits. Missing control
or state remains unknown, and sale prices are not guaranteed merit payouts.

ALL COMMODITIES keeps its full ring scope during market warm-up and presents
the best route per system first. Routes show the original Powerplay source
dates; narrow-window cards retain readable bounds.

Heavy Mining, Journal and Powerplay preparation runs through a shared bounded
background-process service. Available CPU and memory determine concurrency.
Network polling and notifications are batched, and an empty background market
response no longer restarts the whole planner. Server statistics show useful
catalog and synchronization data in place of the permanently empty signal card.

Paired with **catalog server 0.9.3**. Cold large-region reads and broad route
calculations still take time. Partial ring coverage and pending source checks
remain visible; no complete-galaxy coverage or guaranteed speed on every PC is
claimed.

Release validation around HIP 3254 found both comparable Platinum systems and
all 25 supported ALL-system counterparts at 250 and 500 LY. The large populated
Qt test recorded no UI timer gaps above 100 ms during searches or page changes.
Initial loading still had a 3.15-second gap. These are scoped single-PC tests;
see the [validation report](https://github.com/CMDRForcer/ED-Frame/blob/1.0.8/reports/mining_merit_rollout_2026-10-10.md).

**Download:** fully extract `ED-Frame-1.0.8-Windows.zip`, quit your old ED-Frame
including its tray process, then start the new `ED-Frame.exe` with `_internal`
beside it. Existing profiles are reused. Full project source and SHA-256
checksums are separate release assets.

## Deutsch

Gebündelte Abfragen ergänzen passende Verkaufsstationen, die regionale Preis-
Toplisten übergehen. Powerplay-Seiten werden vor der Seitengrenze nach Macht
und Ziel gefiltert. Daten bis 48 Stunden gelten als aktuell; bis 14 Tage alte
Angaben erscheinen mit Originaldatum als „zuletzt bekannt / im Spiel prüfen“.
Fehlende Angaben bleiben unbekannt.

Begrenzte Hintergrundprozesse verteilen schwere Arbeiten nach verfügbaren
Ressourcen. Leere Hintergrundantworten starten keine vollständige Neuberechnung.
ALL COMMODITIES zeigt zunächst die beste Route pro System. Große kalte Suchen
brauchen weiterhin Zeit; die Abdeckung und Quellenzeiten bleiben sichtbar.

Im Vergleich ab HIP 3254 werden bei 250 und 500 LY beide Platinum-Systeme und
alle 25 unterstützten ALL-Vergleichssysteme gefunden. Der große Qt-Test zeigte
während Suche und Seitenwechsel keine UI-Timerpause über 100 ms. Beim ersten
Laden blieb eine Pause von 3,15 Sekunden; weitere Startoptimierung bleibt offen.
