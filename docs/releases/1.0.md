# ED-Frame 1.0

**Your Elite Dangerous Commander workspace — now backed by our own community catalog server.**

1.0 starts the new public ED-Frame release line. It succeeds all earlier EDEC/ED-Frame builds, including 1.5.43, and includes the new regional SQLite mining ring store. Historical tags and downloads remain available; their larger version numbers do not make them newer than this release.

## Download and start

- **Windows portable app:** `ED-Frame-1.0-Windows.zip` — extract the complete ZIP and run `ED-Frame.exe`. Keep `_internal` beside it; no Python installation is needed.
- **Full source project:** `ED-Frame-1.0-Full-Project.zip`.
- **Verify downloads:** `SHA256SUMS.txt` contains SHA-256 checksums for both archives.
- **Platform:** Windows 10/11. **Languages:** English, German, Spanish and French. **License:** GPLv3, free and open source.

Extract into a separate folder. Existing profiles remain under `%LOCALAPPDATA%\ED-Frame`; your personal data is not part of these downloads. The executable is not code-signed.

## The features, individually

- **Operations:** the next collection, trade, unlock, travel or crafting step for your selected build.
- **Engineering:** real ship slots, grade and experimental planning, installed-versus-planned equipment and power estimates.
- **Wishlist:** fleet-wide builds, missing materials and Journal-confirmed progress.
- **Engineers:** searchable capabilities, prerequisites and destinations.
- **Technology Brokers:** one-time Human and Guardian unlock tracking.
- **Materials:** live inventory, farming guidance and build-aware trade planning.
- **Mining Finder:** site evidence, prices, demand, quote age, pad filters and Acquire/Reinforce/Undermine route planning.
- **Measured mining yield:** Prospector/refining evidence alongside clearly distinguished hotspot and ring-type estimates.
- **Module & Ship Finder:** observed station inventories, prices and current-ship/slot compatibility.
- **Commodity Finder:** buying stock or selling demand with quantity, distance, pad and age filters.
- **Station services:** find nearby observed services from Nav.
- **State Finds & HGE:** sightings and reported lifetimes alongside separately labelled BGS predictions.
- **Powerplay:** Journal pledge/rank/merits and explicit system-control observations.
- **CMDR & Fleet:** ranks, reputation, credits, assets, CR/h and known ships, including parked hulls.
- **Missions:** active tasks, deadlines, massacre stacks and recorded Community Goals.
- **Exploration:** unsold scans, noteworthy bodies and estimated cartography values.
- **Exobiology:** survey targets, sampling distances, completed species and observed earnings.
- **Surface navigation:** saved planetary waypoints, compass and a separate overlay.
- **Logbook:** searchable flight events and local notes.
- **Appearance:** six themes, four languages, scaling, service controls and diagnostics.

## Our own server

ED-Frame's public catalog service collects supported EDDN observations and supplies system, station, market, module/ship, ring/hotspot, Powerplay and State Finds data. Supported anonymous mining-yield, signal and station-price contributions extend the shared catalog. Original observation times and explicit checks distinguish current facts, missing evidence and estimates.

The regional SQLite ring store keeps complete original observations and history while reading the search region instead of loading the global ring array. Retained data remains useful offline. Fresh downloads do not make old prices current; observations do not guarantee stock, yield or in-game merits.

**Connection defaults:** ED-Frame and EDDN community services are enabled for new profiles and can be disabled in Connections. The ED-Frame switch controls catalog access and supported anonymous contributions together. Its server does not accept Commander names/FIDs, raw Journals, private builds, wishlists or credentials. Frontier CAPI and INARA require separate setup.
