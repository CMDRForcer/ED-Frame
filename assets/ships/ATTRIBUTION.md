## Anaconda Mk II

`AnacondaMkII.svg` is a new vector illustration created for ED-Frame on
21 October 2026 from the Mk II dorsal and side reference images supplied
by the user. Reference images are not redistributed. This asset is not
from Almanac and is not an official or measured Frontier schematic. It
asserts no technical hardpoint or module positions. Ship design rights
remain with Frontier.

The illustration follows the existing assets: 1200 × 800 transparent canvas,
`#47d7ed`, `currentColor` strokes, 3.2-unit hull outlines, 1.2-unit panel lines,
round joins and 0.16-opacity cyan facets. It consists entirely of native SVG
paths: no embedded raster image, glow, filters or external resources.
Engineering, fleet cards and the ship browser share this same asset.
